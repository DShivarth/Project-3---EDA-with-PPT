💡 Detailed Feature Impact Analysis

1. Memory (Storage) - Highest Impact

  • Phones with 256GB storage command premium prices
  • 128GB is the sweet spot for mid-range phones
  • 64GB and below are budget segment

2. RAM - Second Highest Impact

  • 8GB RAM phones are in premium category
  • 6GB RAM is standard for mid-range
  • 2-4GB RAM indicates budget phones

3. Other Features

  • Battery: Surprisingly low impact on price
  • Camera: Moderate impact, varies by brand
  • Processor: Brand-dependent pricing
